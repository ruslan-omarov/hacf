# Governance

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Author & Lead Maintainer** | Ruslan Omarov | Final decision on releases and Core changes; stewardship of name and direction |
| **Maintainers** | appointed by the Lead Maintainer | Review and merge Indicator and Example changes |
| **Advisory Council** (planned from v1.0) | practitioners, researchers, HR/L&D, legal | Non-binding review of Core proposals and research findings |
| **Contributors** | anyone | Issues, discussions, pull requests, pilot data |

## Decision process by layer

| Layer | Proposal | Review | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | HACF Change Proposal (issue template `core-change`) with rationale and evidence | Public comment ≥ 30 days | Lead Maintainer, after Advisory Council input |
| Indicators | Issue or pull request | ≥ 1 maintainer review; annual review cycle | Maintainers |
| Examples | Pull request | ≥ 1 maintainer review | Maintainers |

Empirical evidence from pilots outranks opinion, including the author's.

## Releases

Tagged releases on GitHub; each release is archived with a DOI. Release notes in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Name and marks

<a id="name-and-marks"></a>
The names "HACF" and "Human–AI Capability Framework" identify this project. The CC BY 4.0 licence permits adaptation, but:

- adapted versions must state that they are modified and must not be presented as the official HACF;
- statements such as "certified by HACF" or "official HACF assessor" may only be used if a certification or assessor programme is formally established and published in this repository;
- "assessed using HACF v0.1.0" (with version) is welcome.

## Independence

HACF is independent of any AI vendor, consultancy or employer. Sponsorship or partnership, if any, will be disclosed here and will not grant influence over the Core.
