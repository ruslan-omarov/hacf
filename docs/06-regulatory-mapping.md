# 06 · Regulatory and Standards Mapping

*Layer: Examples (time-sensitive). Last reviewed: 2026-11. This is not legal advice. Verify current texts with qualified counsel.*

HACF does not create legal compliance on its own. It helps organisations **design, document and evidence** people-related measures that several regimes expect.

## European Union — AI Act, Article 4 (AI literacy)

| Aspect | Status (as of 2026-11) | How HACF helps |
|---|---|---|
| Applicability | Applies since 2 Feb 2025 to providers and deployers of AI systems, regardless of risk tier; extraterritorial reach for systems or outputs used in the EU | Baseline L1 for everyone exposed to AI |
| Nature of duty | Amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026) from ensuring "a sufficient level" to taking measures that **support the development** of AI literacy — an obligation of effort | Structured, role-based programme = demonstrable effort |
| Measurement | The Commission states there is no obligation to measure employees' AI knowledge and no certificate is needed | HACF assessment is **voluntary** and used for targeting, not as a legal test |
| Records | Internal records of measures are considered acceptable evidence | Assessment logs, role profiles, training plans, re-assessment cycle |
| Proportionality | Expectations scale with role, context and system | Role profiles + proportionality modifiers ([05](05-role-profiles.md)) |
| Human oversight of high-risk systems (Art. 14, 26) | Deployers must assign oversight to competent persons | Operator profile: J≥4, R≥4 |

## Kazakhstan — Law No. 230-VIII "On Artificial Intelligence"

Signed 17 Nov 2025, in force since 18 Jan 2026. Key elements relevant to people capability:

| Element of the law | People-capability implication | HACF link |
|---|---|---|
| Priority of the human; preservation of human autonomy and informed decision-making | People overseeing AI must be able to evaluate and override it | J dimension; L3+ gates |
| Classification of AI systems by risk (minimal, medium, high) and by autonomy | Higher risk/autonomy → stronger competence for users and owners | Proportionality modifiers |
| Distributed responsibility of owners, possessors and users; continuous control and risk management | Users are accountable, not only vendors | R dimension; L4 controls; L5 governance |
| Labelling of synthetic content that may mislead | Content creators must know and apply labelling rules | R≥3 for content producers |
| Data protection and confidentiality requirements | Correct tool choice by data class | R≥2 for all regular users |

The **Digital Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan** (No. 255-VIII, in force since 12 Jul 2026) adds, among other things, a right to review decisions made by automated systems and AI — relevant for staff who handle such reviews (J≥3, R≥3).

## ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management systems

| Clause | Requirement theme | HACF evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 7.2 Competence | Determine necessary competence, ensure it, retain documented evidence | Role profiles, assessment records, development plans |
| 7.3 Awareness | Persons aware of AI policy and their contribution | L1 baseline; R1 |
| 9.1 Monitoring, measurement | Evaluate performance | HACF Index, coverage, risk exposure trends |
