# 07 · Crosswalk to Other Frameworks

*Layer: Examples. Mappings are approximate and indicative; the referenced frameworks have their own scope, purpose and terminology. Verify against the original sources before formal use.*

HACF is designed to **interoperate**, not to replace. An organisation already using DigComp or SFIA can keep it and add HACF where those frameworks do not reach: workplace value creation with AI, building, and gated judgment/responsibility.

## Positioning

| Framework | Unit assessed | Main question | Relation to HACF |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DigComp 3.0** (EU JRC, Nov 2025) | Citizen | Digital competence across 21 competences, with AI integrated transversally | Broad digital base; HACF zooms into AI at work |
| **SFIA** (skills framework and its AI-related guidance) | Professional | Skills and responsibility levels for IT/digital work | HACF levels can be attached to SFIA skill profiles |
| **[Operational framework for AI literacy in the workplace](https://www.interface-eu.org/publications/ai-literacy-workplace-framework)** (interface, 2025) | Employee | Literacy duties by role: baseline, operational, oversight | Closely aligned with HACF L1–L3 and the J dimension |
| **ASEAN AI literacy framework** | Citizen/learner | Basic → advanced use, adaptation, creation | Regional reference for education pipelines |
| **Vendor certifications** (e.g. cloud AI practitioner/developer exams) | Individual | Knowledge of a vendor's AI services | Evidence for K and C (E2/E3), never a level by itself |
| **Organisational AI maturity models** (e.g. cloud-provider generative AI maturity models) | Organisation | Readiness to adopt and scale AI | Paired with HACF via the enablement check ([04](04-enterprise.md)) |

## Indicative level alignment

| HACF | DigComp 3.0 proficiency | Workplace AI literacy (interface) | Typical certification evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 Unaware | below Basic | — | — |
| L1 Aware | Basic | Baseline | Awareness course completion |
| L2 User | Intermediate | Operational | Foundational AI certificate (K evidence) |
| L3 Practitioner | Advanced | Operational → Oversight | Practitioner-level certificate + practical task |
| L4 Builder | Advanced → Highly advanced | Oversight (for own solutions) | Developer/engineer-level certificate + artefact |
| L5 Leader | Highly advanced | Oversight (organisational) | Strategy/leadership programmes + outcome evidence |

## Dimension alignment

| HACF dimension | DigComp 3.0 competence areas (indicative) |
|---|---|
| K Knowledge | AI-related knowledge statements across areas |
| A Application | 1 Information; 2 Communication & collaboration; 3 Content creation |
| C Creation | 3 Content creation; 5 Problem identification and solving |
| J Judgment | 1 Information search, evaluation and management |
| R Responsibility | 4 Safety, wellbeing and responsible use |

## Using HACF with a job architecture

Attach a target HACF profile to each role in your existing job architecture (SFIA, a corporate competency model, or grade structure). HACF then becomes one column in the competency matrix — not a parallel system.
